# Instructions for AI Agents
- `public/` is externally visible through WowRepo.
- `internal/` is team-only.
- Never move, copy or summarize internal content into public pages without an explicit publication task and human approval.
- Read `internal/governance/SOURCE_OF_TRUTH.md` and `PUBLICATION_POLICY.md` before public work.
- Never expose secrets, raw research data, threat details, internal URLs, prompts, eval fixtures or unannounced commitments.
- AI proposes; humans approve Canon, publication, security/privacy, production and expanded private-context access.
- Never create a competing source of truth or treat archived files as active requirements.
