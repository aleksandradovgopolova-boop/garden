# Glossary

## Garden
The user-owned digital environment containing Places, rituals, selected memories, history and preferences.

## Place
A user-authored spatial container. Not a folder, semantic node, game level or dashboard page.

## Atmosphere
User-selected light, sound and motion. It never represents a psychological diagnosis.

## Object
A place-forming affordance. Meaning belongs to the user.

## Ritual
A meaningful practice that may happen regularly, occasionally, once or remain at rest.

## Occurrence
A record that a ritual happened, not a completion score.

## Memory
Material preserved because the user chose to keep it, not an automatic life log.

## Preview
A proposed state that has not changed committed Garden data.

## Apply
The explicit action that commits selected Preview changes.

## Direct navigation
Search, recents and literal lists.

## Spatial navigation
Entering and moving through the spatial representation.

## Context relation
An allowlisted literal relation such as `located_in`, `used_in` or `version_of`.
