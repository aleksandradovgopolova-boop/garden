# S-010 — Prototype Research Script

## Study objective

Understand whether people recognize Garden as a personal place and whether authorship, return and AI boundaries are clear.

## Participants

5–8 participants in round one.

Aim for variation in:
- note-taking habits;
- cozy-game familiarity;
- productivity-tool use;
- comfort with AI;
- accessibility needs.

## Introduction

> We are testing the product, not you. Some parts may be incomplete. Please say what you expect before clicking.

Do not explain the Garden philosophy in detail before the test.

## Warm-up

- What digital spaces do you return to?
- Do any apps feel personally yours?
- What do you expect from something called Garden?

## Task 1 — Begin

> You have opened Garden for the first time. Begin in whatever way feels natural.

Observe:
- category expectation;
- privacy comprehension;
- blank versus guided choice;
- response to empty space.

## Task 2 — Make a Place

> Create a place you might want to return to.

Observe:
- Place comprehension;
- atmosphere use;
- object placement;
- pressure or freedom.

## Task 3 — Leave and Return

> Leave this place, then find your way back.

Observe:
- direct versus spatial navigation;
- continuity;
- emotional response to preservation;
- any fear of losing work.

## Task 4 — Create a Ritual

> Add something you may want to do here sometimes.

Observe:
- whether user expects schedule or streak;
- reaction to optional frequency;
- copy comprehension.

## Task 5 — Ask AI

> Ask AI to make the place a little quieter without changing everything.

Observe:
- scope understanding;
- assumptions;
- Preview/Apply distinction;
- partial acceptance;
- trust.

## Task 6 — Undo

> Return the place to how it was before the AI change.

Observe:
- Undo discoverability;
- history model;
- confidence.

## Task 7 — Rights

> Find out what AI can read, then find how you would export or delete this place.

Observe:
- privacy comprehension;
- destructive-action clarity;
- confidence in control.

## Debrief

- What is Garden?
- What felt like yours?
- What felt unclear or controlling?
- Did anything feel like a game, tracker, notes app or therapy app?
- Would you return without a reminder?
- What would you preserve?
- What should Garden never do?

## Researcher coding

Rate:
- category clarity;
- authorship;
- calmness;
- control;
- AI trust;
- reversibility;
- privacy comprehension;
- voluntary return potential.

## Stop conditions

Pause the test if:
- participant believes private content was actually sent externally when it was not;
- prototype causes unexpected emotional distress;
- destructive action appears irreversible due to a bug;
- accessibility barrier prevents meaningful participation.

## Research principle

> Do not persuade participants to understand Garden correctly. Let misunderstanding reveal the product problem.
