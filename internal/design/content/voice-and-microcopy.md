# S-003 — Content and Microcopy

## Voice

Garden speaks literally, quietly and respectfully.

It does not:
- praise compliance;
- imply disappointment;
- diagnose;
- dramatize;
- mystify system behavior;
- anthropomorphize the Garden to create obligation.

## Welcome

Headline:
> Begin with one place.

Body:
> Garden is a private digital place you can shape and return to. Nothing else is required.

Actions:
- Begin empty
- Help me begin

Privacy note:
> Your Garden is private by default.

## Empty Place

Preferred:
> There is room here.

Supporting:
> Add something, change the atmosphere, or leave it as it is.

Rejected:
- Your Garden is empty
- Complete your space
- Start growing
- Unlock your potential

## AI Request

Label:
> What would you like to change?

Scope:
> AI can read only the place and items selected below.

Action:
> Create a preview

Rejected:
- Let Garden understand you
- Improve my space
- Make this feel more like me

## Assumptions

Heading:
> What AI assumed

Body:
> These assumptions are not changes. Edit or remove any of them before continuing.

## Preview

Heading:
> Preview

Status:
> Nothing has changed yet.

Actions:
- Apply selected changes
- Reject
- Back to request

## Version receipt

> Changes saved. You can undo this version.

## Undo

> Version undone. The previous state is restored in a new version.

## Ritual

Create:
> Create a ritual

Description:
> A ritual may happen regularly, occasionally, once, or not for a while.

Occurrence:
> Record that this happened

Rejected:
- Complete ritual
- Keep your streak
- Stay consistent
- Do not lose momentum

## Reminder

> You asked to be reminded about “Evening Reading”.

## Inactivity

No copy is shown.

## Memory

Create:
> Preserve a memory

Supporting:
> Add only what you want to keep.

## Delete

Heading:
> Delete this place?

Body:
> The place and its direct contents will be removed. Linked memories and rituals will remain unless you choose to delete them separately.

Actions:
- Cancel
- Export first
- Delete place

## Offline

> You are offline. Your draft is kept on this device and has not been saved to your Garden yet.

## Failed save

> The change was not saved. Your draft is still available.

## AI failure

> A preview could not be created. Your Garden has not changed.

## Content principle

> Copy explains product state. It does not assign meaning to the person.
