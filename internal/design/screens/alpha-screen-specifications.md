# V-010 — Initial Screen Specifications

## Welcome
One title, one explanation, two choices and a privacy note. No carousel or feature grid.

## First Place Setup
Place preview plus atmosphere controls. No progress meter.

## Place View
Dominant world surface, quiet navigation, Add, Edit, Ask AI and contextual inspector. No persistent dashboard sidebar.

## Object Library
Literal names, thumbnails and accessibility descriptions. No rarity or locked states.

## Ritual Editor
Title, description, place, optional reminder and lifecycle. No streak.

## AI Request
Selected scope, readable context and Create Preview.

## AI Preview
Before/after, atomic change list and persistent message: “Nothing has changed yet.”

## History
Source, author, timestamp, provenance, inspect and restore preview.

## Privacy and Data
Ownership, AI access, sessions, export and deletion.

## Garden Home
Current Place, recent Places, search and spatial entrance. No feed or recommendations.

> Initial screens should prove clarity, authorship and return before visual richness.
