# Visual Foundations

Quiet Spatial Modernism + Soft Domestic Presence + Atmospheric Abstraction.

---

## Imported source: `V-001_visual_territories_v0_1.md`

# V-001 — Visual Territories

Garden combines three visual territories.

## Quiet Spatial Modernism
Architectural calm, clear geometry, restrained surfaces, negative space and light as orientation.

## Soft Domestic Presence
Human scale, familiar objects, tactile warmth and subtle imperfection without decorative excess.

## Atmospheric Abstraction
Light, haze, depth and sound create place without demanding full realism.

## Recommended direction
Use modernist structure, domestic intimacy and abstract atmosphere.

Avoid:
- cozy-game inventory;
- fantasy world UI;
- productivity dashboard;
- mystical wellness;
- social feed;
- smart-home control panel.

> Garden should feel inhabitable without pretending to be a complete world.

# V-002 — References and Anti-references

Use references by quality, not imitation.

Look for:
- thresholds and framed views;
- light as orientation;
- editorial hierarchy;
- quiet digital instruments;
- ambient media without reward.

Anti-references:
- Animal Crossing inventory;
- Stardew Valley task loop;
- Tamagotchi care pressure;
- Notion workspace;
- Obsidian graph;
- Pinterest moodboard;
- meditation gradients;
- AI sparkle;
- metaverse lobby;
- SaaS dashboard.

Every reference must be checked for authorship, pressure, progression, accessibility and clarity of system state.

# V-003 — Color System

Color creates atmosphere but never diagnoses emotion.

Core roles:
- canvas;
- surface;
- raised surface;
- primary and secondary text;
- borders;
- focus;
- action;
- success;
- warning;
- danger;
- selection;
- preview.

Direction:
- mineral neutrals;
- muted warm and cool variants;
- restrained botanical accents;
- one functional accent;
- one preview color;
- one danger color.

Avoid neon AI purple, reward green, fantasy jewel palettes and beige-on-beige ambiguity.

All states require WCAG-compliant contrast and non-color indicators.

## Imported source: `V-004_typography_v0_1.md`

# V-004 — Typography

Use a highly legible contemporary sans serif for navigation, controls, history, privacy and AI scope.

An optional restrained serif or humanist text face may be used for longer user-authored memories.

Rules:
- no mystical display type in functional UI;
- no all-caps primary hierarchy;
- readable minimum size;
- 45–75 character reading width;
- visible attribution of AI-generated text;
- user-authored content visually outranks system explanation.

> Typography should help the user hear their own words before hearing the system.

## Imported source: `V-005_spatial_language_v0_1.md`

# V-005 — Spatial Language

Primitives:
- field;
- threshold;
- layer;
- anchor;
- opening;
- path;
- cluster;
- edge;
- depth;
- pause.

A Place uses one dominant field, a small number of anchors, clear negative space and readable foreground/background.

Avoid collectible grids, icon carpets, hidden hotspots and game maps.

Every spatial composition has an equal structured representation:

Place
- Atmosphere
- Objects
- Rituals
- Memories

> Space provides orientation, not hierarchy of personal importance.

## Imported source: `V-006_ui_surfaces_v0_1.md`

# V-006 — UI Surfaces

## World Surface
The Place itself. Visually primary and directly manipulable.

## Context Surface
Selected object, ritual or memory. Compact, contextual and dismissible.

## System Surface
History, privacy, export, deletion and settings. Literal and stable.

## Proposal Surface
AI scope, assumptions, Preview and Diff. Visibly separate from committed state.

Only one surface should dominate at a time.

> The interface must reveal whether the user is inhabiting, editing or operating the system.

# V-007 — Motion

Motion supports continuity and orientation.

Allowed:
- entering or leaving a Place;
- object movement;
- atmosphere transition;
- Preview comparison;
- panel opening;
- save and sync state.

Forbidden:
- confetti;
- streak celebration;
- pulsing retention prompts;
- AI magic sparkle;
- animated guilt;
- distracting idle loops.

Reduced motion removes parallax, zoom and ambient movement while preserving state through labels and focus management.

> Motion shows continuity. It does not manufacture excitement.
