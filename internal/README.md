# Garden Internal Repository
This layer is for the team and AI OPS.

- `product/` — active requirements and models
- `design/` — interaction, visual, content and accessibility contracts
- `engineering/` — architecture, security and AI OPS
- `decisions/` — GDRs and ADRs
- `research/` — operational studies, scripts, audits and market work
- `delivery/` — current cycle, backlog and quality gates
- `governance/` — contribution rules, glossary, sources of truth and audits

Internal documents are not rendered by WowRepo.
