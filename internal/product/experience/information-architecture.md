# P-001 — Information Architecture

Garden is organized around lived context, not files, dashboards or a knowledge graph.

## Top-level areas
- Garden
- Places
- Rituals
- Memories
- History
- Settings

## Navigation
Two equal systems:
1. Spatial navigation through places and objects.
2. Direct navigation through search, recent places and explicit links.

## Alpha tree
```text
Garden Home
├── Current Place
├── Places
├── Rituals
├── Memories
├── History
└── Settings
```

## Rules
- no feed;
- no workspace tree;
- no graph view;
- no dashboard of self;
- no completion overview;
- no automatic related-content rail.

Use literal labels: Place, Object, Ritual, Memory, History, Preview, Apply, Undo, Export, Delete.

> Garden organizes by lived context, not semantic similarity.
